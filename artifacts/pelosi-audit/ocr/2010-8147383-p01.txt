RE: Amendment to Speaker Pelosi’s 2009 Financial Disclosure
Dear Madam Clerk:

Please accept this letter as explanation for the amendment to Speaker Pelosi’s 2009
Financial Disclosure Statement.

Attached is amended “Schedule IV — Transactions” representing pages 14 and 15 of
Speaker Pelosi's filing. The schedule is amended with changes only to the column
entitled “Capital Gain in Excess of $200?” We originally incorrectly interpreted the
column as requiring a “Yes” response to any transaction “in excess of $200”. We have
properly revised the column to reflect the fact that of the thirteen sale transactions, eleven
were losses and two were gains.

Please call if you have any questions regarding the above.
