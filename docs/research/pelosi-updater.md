## Behavior

Every run rebuilds an overlay from the official annual indexes starting at 2025 and PDFs for new PTR documents. Reviewed filings remain the fixed starting ledger. Source document IDs, URLs, pages and hashes are retained. Explicit spouse-owned stock purchases and sales and fully described call purchases/sales are supported. New stock purchases assume no undisclosed opening position. New share quantities shift both bounds of modeled ranges. Full known sales remove zero positions from the displayed stock list. Block's XYZ symbol maps to the existing SQ position.

Runs never append to existing balances; duplicate event IDs and excessive sales fail closed. Any new annual/amended filing, missing known filing, historical/future date, ambiguous quantity, unrecognized asset or owner, exercise, gift or corporate action prevents publication of a partial overlay. The application falls back to the reviewed ledger with a visible notice. New annual baselines and ambiguous filings still require review; this is not fully autonomous interpretation of arbitrary disclosures. Corporate actions are not automatically maintained by this worker.

No user notification or email is generated by the worker. Existing workflow failure handling is unchanged. Snapshot reads are public; writes are restricted to the service role. Missing/unavailable snapshots and checks older than 36 hours (24-hour cadence plus scheduling grace) show a status warning. A current snapshot does not imply that undisclosed transactions are known.

Validation: parser fixtures cover buys, sales, calls, ambiguous quantities, amendments and multiple rows. Backend tests cover range roll-forward, new stocks, closed positions, duplicate prevention, ticker renames and excessive sales. Live dry run verifies the present official inventory.
